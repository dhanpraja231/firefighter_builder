# Characters and roles

Each entry: name, role in the story, short description, relationships.

Names are still to be picked, so entries use their role for now.

## Robots

All the robots and the commander share the same calm, professional, procedural voice. What differs is their job, not
their personality.

### Commander (AI)

- **Role:** runs the rescue from outside the building.
- **Description:** assigns floors and tasks, sets priorities, re-plans after twists.
- **Relationships:** gives orders to all three robots and reports to the human, who judges the outcome.

### Scout drone

- **Role:** eyes of the team.
- **Description:** sweeps floors, finds victims and hot spots, reports back. Its overhead shots are the documentary's wide shots.
- **Relationships:** reports to the commander. Its findings decide where the rescue robot goes.

### Hose robot

- **Role:** keeps the building survivable.
- **Description:** fights the fire and keeps the stairwell and hallways passable.
- **Relationships:** clears the routes the rescue robot needs. It is the first to react to the stairwell collapse.

### Rescue robot

- **Role:** gets people out.
- **Description:** reaches victims, talks them into leaving, guides or carries them out. Most of the dialogue with victims is its.
- **Relationships:** in direct contact with every victim. Depends on the scout to find them and on the hose robot for a route.

## Victims

There are 6 units, 8 people in all. Pairs are separate agents with their own memory, but they start together and try to
stay together. Each victim remembers what they have seen and what the robots told them, and reacts to it.

### Elderly resident

- **Role:** the rescue that takes patience.
- **Description:** stubborn and polite, has lived in the building for decades. Won't leave without their pet (pet to be decided).
- **Relationships:** knows most neighbors, so may tell the robots where others are.

### Parent

- **Role:** the rescue that goes wrong.
- **Description:** panicked, runs the wrong way looking for the child.
- **Relationships:** the child's parent. If the child goes missing (twist), searching for them is all the parent can think about.

### Child

- **Role:** the twist.
- **Description:** young and scared. Can slip away before reaching the exit.
- **Relationships:** follows the parent, and may trust the rescue robot more than the parent does.

### Skeptic

- **Role:** the argument with the machine.
- **Description:** distrusts robots, wants a human firefighter, refuses help and argues.
- **Relationships:** may be won over by seeing the robot save someone else.

### Teen

- **Role:** the outside view.
- **Description:** filming everything on their phone and streaming the fire live. Won't leave because of the stream.
- **Relationships:** their footage can show up as extra shots in the documentary.

### Regular resident

- **Role:** the baseline.
- **Description:** ordinary and cooperative, follows instructions. Shows what an easy rescue looks like, to compare with the others.
- **Relationships:** may help the robots by guiding others or calming the parent.

### Couple (two agents)

- **Role:** the rescue that can't be split.
- **Description:** an ordinary couple who want to stay together. Splitting them up slows the rescue.
- **Relationships:** each other. If one is hurt or stuck, the other refuses to go.
